Privacy First

Your Data Never Leaves Your Device

We built SBETS-LITE with a non-negotiable principle: your financial data is yours alone.

🚫

No Bank Login Required

We never ask for your credentials. SBETS-LITE works purely from SMS your bank already sends.

📵

No SMS Stored on Servers

Your messages are read locally, parsed on-device, and immediately discarded — never transmitted.

💾

Local Storage Only

All transaction data is saved in local device storage. Uninstall and it’s gone completely.

🔐

Open Source

Anyone can audit the code on GitHub to verify our privacy claims. Zero hidden endpoints.
